## What NOT to sell

**The lead lists.** The ECOWAS clinic CSV and the 63-institution APSFD tracker are valuable to you, but reselling personal contact data creates real legal exposure — GDPR reaches EU-resident data subjects wherever you operate, and several ECOWAS states now have data protection authorities with registration requirements. Use them internally.

A *market map* — institution counts, categories, regulatory landscape, no named individuals — is a legitimate product at around $149. Different artefact, worth building.
